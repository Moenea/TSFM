Heterogeneous-TSFM Fusion  ·  Overall Framework  ·  V1 Dataflow

A context-gated fusion of four heterogeneous forecasters
for early fault prognosis on XMEAS10

Four experts each forecast the Purge Rate over a 15-step horizon; a small MLP reads only the context of the signal and emits per-step softmax weights that blend them. The gate never sees the experts' predictions — it decides whom to trust from the state of the process alone.
